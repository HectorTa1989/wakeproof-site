ShakeWake Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026 · Applies to the ShakeWake apps for Android and iOS

ShakeWake ("we", "the app") is published by Long Hung Ta, an independent developer in Vietnam. Contact: hectorta1989@outlook.com.

The short version

  • No account and no sign-up. We don't run a server that stores your data.
  • Your alarms, wake-up missions, wake history, XP, streaks and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The camera is used only during a QR-code or photo mission. Images are analysed on your device and are never saved or uploaded.
  • On Android, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob (the iOS app has no ads), and purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple through RevenueCat. These services receive the limited data described below.

Data stored on your device

Alarm settings (time, label, repeat days, mission, sound), wake records (date, time, success, completion time, XP) and preferences. You can delete all of it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. This data is excluded from device backups or, where backup is enabled by your operating system, stays inside your own Google/Apple backup.

Camera

With your permission, the camera is used during QR-code and photo missions. Frames are processed in memory by Google ML Kit (Android) or Apple Vision / ML Kit (iOS) on the device to detect a barcode or recognise an object. No image leaves the device.

Advertising choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The iOS app shows no ads and does not track you. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying ShakeWake Pro removes ads.

Children

ShakeWake is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and the app stores are retained as required for billing, tax and restoring purchases. To ask for deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous app user ID, email us from the device's store account and we will forward the request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we don't hold your data on our own servers, most requests can be met by deleting the app data. For anything else, contact us at the email above.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
