ShakeWake Support

The alarm that won't stop until you're really up.

Questions, bugs or refund help: email hectorta1989@outlook.com. We usually reply within two working days.

My alarm didn't ring

  • iPhone: allow notifications for ShakeWake (Settings → Notifications → ShakeWake), turn on Time Sensitive Notifications, and keep the ringer on. Focus modes can silence alarms unless ShakeWake is allowed.
  • Android: allow notifications and "Alarms & reminders", and turn on full-screen alarms. ShakeWake shows a warning on the home screen when one of these is off.

ShakeWake Pro

  • Restore a purchase: Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change your plan: Settings → Manage Subscription, or your Apple Account → Subscriptions / Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

ShakeWake has no account and stores your alarms and history only on your device. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
